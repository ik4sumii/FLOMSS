Read abstract

We present a systematic study of flow matching for music source separation in full-band stereo. The study investigates prediction targets, flow paths, source distribution factorization, and mixture consistency, and evaluates the perception–distortion tradeoff using signal, spectral, and multi-embedding perceptual metrics.
